Our services

01

Spine & Back Pain Treatment

Effective physiotherapy for cervical pain, back pain, disc bulge, sciatica, headaches, and posture-related problems.

02

Joint & Muscle Pain Management

Treatment for shoulder pain, frozen shoulder, knee pain, knee osteoarthritis, tennis elbow, golfer's elbow, and ankle sprains.

03

Sports Injury & Rehabilitation

Personalized rehabilitation for IT band syndrome, plantar fasciitis, sports injuries, and post-surgical recovery, including TKR and THR rehabilitation.

04

Advanced Pain Relief Therapies

Specialized treatments including IASTM, cupping therapy, wet cupping (Hijama), electrotherapy, dry needling, and manual therapy.

05

Exercise Therapy & Functional Rehabilitation

Customized exercise programs, sports taping, mobility training, and strengthening exercises to improve flexibility, stability, and physical function.
